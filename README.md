# Junli Liu

Ph.D. student at Northwestern Polytechnical University.

My research focuses on **Embodied AI**, **Vision-Language Navigation**, and **World Models**.

## Selected Work

- **[AerialVG](https://github.com/Ideal-ljl/AerialVG)** · ICCV 2025  
  A benchmark for aerial visual grounding with an emphasis on positional relations and spatial reasoning. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07836)
- **[OpenFly](https://github.com/SHAILAB-IPEC/OpenFly-Platform)** · ICLR 2026  
  A platform and benchmark for aerial vision-language navigation. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18041)
- **[FreeGaussian](https://github.com/Tavish9/freegaussian)** · AAAI 2026  
  Annotation-free control of articulated objects using 3D Gaussian splats and flow derivatives. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.22070)
